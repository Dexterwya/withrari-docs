Aurivelle theme documentation

Aurivelle is a Shopify theme for beauty, skincare and wellness brands. It's built for product-led stores: bundles, multi-pack offers, before-and-after stories and lots of social proof.

Support: info@scalewithrari.com (we reply within two business days).

1. Getting started

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes, add Aurivelle, and click Customize.
  2. Open Theme settings (the gear icon) and set your Colors and Typography, then upload your logo in the Header section. Every section picks these up automatically.
  3. Go through the home page sections from top to bottom and replace the demo images, text and products.
  4. Assign the product templates (see section 5) to the products that need them.
  5. Preview on mobile with the phone icon in the editor before you publish.

3. Header and footer

  • Announcement bar: add one message per block. Turn on Scroll messages across the bar for a moving strip. It pauses on hover and keyboard focus, and stays still for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • Header: logo, main menu, search, account and cart. Upload your logo in the Header section. Choose a layout under Theme settings → Layout and style → Header layout: logo centered, logo centered and stacked, logo left, or minimal with a drawer menu.
  • Footer: logo, a short description, menu columns, newsletter signup, payment icons, country and language selectors, and Shopify's "Powered by" link.

4. Sections

Add sections with Add section in the editor. Each section has its own background (page, card, tint or brand) and top and bottom spacing.

Hero and banners

  • Hero slideshow: full-width slides with heading, text, two buttons and progress bars. Show labels above the progress bars is optional.
  • Image banner: one large image with heading, text and buttons. Put it first on a page to get the transparent header.
  • Promo tiles: two or more image tiles linking to collections or pages.
  • Promo bar: a slim offer strip. It can stick to the top of the screen once shoppers scroll to it.
  • Scrolling text: a moving strip of short messages. You can adjust the space between messages; icons and the pause button are optional.

Products and collections

  • Product grid: hand-picked product cards with badges, ratings, hover images and quick add.
  • Collection tabs: switch between collections without leaving the page.
  • Featured product: a full buy box on any page, with multi-pack offers (1, 2 or 3 units), benefits and an image gallery.
  • Bundle: two products plus a set price, shown as "A + B = set".
  • Shop the look: a photo with numbered product hotspots.
  • Collection list: collection cards in a grid or slider.
  • Collage: a mix of image, product, collection and video tiles.

Social proof

  • Social proof: rating, two numbers and a quote, in a row.
  • Customer reviews: a review wall with scrolling quote tickers.
  • Testimonials: a quote slider with star ratings and a linked product.
  • Review highlights and Reviews list: a rating breakdown with reviews.
  • Results with numbers, Results band and Key numbers: survey results and stats. Always add the footnote that explains where the numbers come from.
  • Logo list and In the press: press logos and quotes.

Story and education

  • Photo with steps, Photo steps and Timeline: how-to and routine content.
  • Ingredients carousel, Ingredient list and Ingredient grid: ingredient spotlights.
  • Image comparison: a drag slider to compare two images, such as before and after or morning and night.
  • Comparison table: your product compared with typical alternatives.
  • Benefits around product, Skin tones and Coverage tabs: made for makeup and complexion products.
  • Image with text, Multicolumn, Rich text, Icon bar, Trust icons, Team, Video, Image gallery.

Engagement

  • Collapsible content and FAQ: questions and answers, with an optional image.
  • Newsletter and Newsletter banner: email signup, connected to Shopify customers.
  • Blog posts: the latest articles from a blog, 2 to 4 per row.
  • Contact form section and Custom Liquid.

5. Product pages

Aurivelle ships four product templates. Assign one to a product under Products → [product] → Theme template.

TemplateBest for
Default productAny product.
product.serumHero skincare: benefits, timeline, results, steps, comparison table, ingredients and FAQ.
product.cushionMakeup: shade swatches, coverage tabs, benefits around the product, ingredients and a bundle card.
product.bundleSets and kits: what's in the set, routine steps and results.

Multi-pack offers

In Product information, set up to three pack options (for example 1, 2 and 3 bottles) with labels, a "Most popular" flag and the savings line. Each pack adds that quantity of the selected variant to the cart.

Ratings

Cards and product pages read ratings from your reviews app automatically (through Shopify's standard product reviews metafields). The Rating and Count fields in sections are only a fallback for when no reviews app is installed.

Complementary products

The Complementary products section uses Shopify's free Search & Discovery app. Set complementary products there, and the section appears on the product page automatically. It stays hidden until you do.

6. Cart

The cart opens as a drawer from the header icon or after Add to cart. Shoppers can change quantities and remove items there, or on the cart page. The cart drawer's title, delivery note and checkout button label are set in the Cart drawer section (open it from the editor's sidebar), along with an optional order note and express checkout buttons.

7. Accessibility and performance

  • All interactive elements work with the keyboard and have visible focus styles.
  • Motion stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • Images load lazily below the fold and use responsive sizes. Upload images at least 2000px wide for banners and 1500px for products.

8. FAQ

How do I change the cart icon? Theme settings → Layout and style → Cart icon.

Why is the header white text over my image? The first section is an image banner or slideshow, so the header becomes transparent. Turn this off under Theme settings → Header.

How many sections can a page have? Shopify allows up to 25 sections per page.

Can I translate the theme? Yes. Go to Online Store → Themes → … → Edit default theme content.

Where do I get help? Email info@scalewithrari.com with your store URL and a screenshot.
